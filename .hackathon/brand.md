**Brand phase complete:** 2026-09-19

All names are:
- Easy to pronounce (1–3 syllables)
- Available (not single-company trademarks)
- Aligned to core product function

## Recommended name

### **SENTINEL**

**Why:** Already the working name; perfectly captures "guard standing watch over exams"; memorable, easy to pronounce, distinctive in tech; aligns to the control-tower metaphor and real-time detection mission. No competing trademark dominates this word. Highest brand equity among the five.

---

## One-liner (≤12 words)

**"Real-time exam integrity with transparent fairness verdicts"** (7 words)

Alternative: "The control tower that keeps exams fair, transparent, and trustworthy" (11 words)

---

## 3-word tagline

### **"See. Protect. Prove."**

- **See** — real-time detection of anomalies before candidates notice
- **Protect** — freeze sessions, checkpoint responses, shield candidates from harm
- **Prove** — transparent audit trail and auditable fairness verdict

---

## Tone (3 adjectives)

1. **Vigilant** — always watching, proactive, present; no anomaly escapes notice
2. **Transparent** — shows its reasoning; policy-driven verdicts; no black-box decisions
3. **Trustworthy** — authoritative without arrogance; grounds exam admin in evidence, not assertions

## Color direction

**Accent:** Forest Green (#2D5016 or similar deep, natural green)
- Conveys integrity, vigilance, and watchfulness
- Natural and grounded (not cold like navy)
- Suggests growth and honest accountability

**Neutrals:** White, warm gray (#999999), dark charcoal (#333333)
- Creates professional, modern, tech-forward palette
- Pairs well with forest green for readable, sophisticated look
- No rainbow; 1 accent + 3 neutrals

**Palette use:**
- Primary accent: forest green for highlights, buttons, active states
- Background: white or warm gray
- Text/structure: dark charcoal
- Subtle accents: warm gray for secondary UI elements

---

## Logo text treatment (wordmark only)

**Format:** Text-only wordmark; no separate pictorial logo.

**Typography:**
- Font: Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat, Inter, Nunito Sans, or custom)
- Weight: Medium or semi-bold for authority and solidity
- Letter-spacing: Slightly open (relaxed geometric feel, not cramped)
- Style: Upright, no italics

**Optional geometric integration:**
- Thin horizontal accent line above or below the wordmark (suggests order, stability)
- Minimal geometric device integrated into a single letter (e.g., subtle guard/beacon symbol within the "S"), if it does not compromise legibility
- **Absolute rule:** Logo is word-first; any geometric element is subordinate, not a separate mark

**Application:**
- Works at small scales (favicon ~16px, app icon ~256px)
- Renders clearly in single color (forest green on white, white on charcoal, etc.)
- Monochrome version available for restricted backgrounds

## Brand essence

**Sentinel** is the control tower that makes exam integrity visible, trustworthy, and auditable.

- **Vision:** Exams that are inherently fair because their integrity is monitored in real time and verdicts are transparent.
- **Promise:** See what is happening to your exams, protect your candidates with evidence-based decisions, prove fairness with an unbroken audit chain.
- **Archetype:** The Guard (watchful, protective, authoritative).

---

## Notes for architects and designers

1. **Wordmark should feel tech-forward but not futuristic.** This is production infrastructure, not a startup time-travel simulator. Confidence and solidity matter more than novelty.
2. **Forest green was chosen over navy** to avoid the cold, sterile control-room cliché. Integrity should feel warm, grounded, and alive.
3. **The 3-word tagline "See. Protect. Prove." maps to the product loop:** See anomalies → Protect candidates → Prove fairness. Use it in onboarding.
4. **"Transparent" in the tone is non-negotiable.** Every verdict card must render its inputs, thresholds, and arithmetic. No hidden decision logic.
